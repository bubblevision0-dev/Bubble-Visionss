System Information

Powered by Machine Learning

BubbleVision utilizes a K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classification algorithm. Instead of simple light-thresholding, the system "recognizes" patterns of shaded ink by comparing them to thousands of pre-verified samples.

KNN Classifier
Pattern-based recognition
Noise Filtering
Ignores dust and stray marks

Managing Students

1

Single Entry: Add students individually by entering their Grade Level, Section, First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Gender in the 'Students' dashboard.

Bulk Import (PDF/Word): You can upload an entire class list at once. Please ensure your file follows this exact format:

        Male
            1. [Student Name]
            2. [Student Name]

        Female
            1. [Student Name]
            2. [Student Name]
The system uses AI to parse these headers and categorize students automatically.

2. Scanning & Assessment

2

Setup Answer Key: Provide the Assessment Name, Year Level, Section, and Subject.

Upload Key: (PDF, Word, or CSV) following this format:

1. A
2. B
3. C ...
3

Auto-Generate Bubble Sheet: Once the key is uploaded, BubbleVision automatically creates a custom OMR sheet.

The number of bubbles will match exactly the number of items in your key.
4

Capture & Upload: Use your camera to upload the completed sheets.

Important: Ensure the four corner boxes are clearly visible in your photo. These markers serve as the alignment guide for the KNN Algorithm to accurately locate bubbles.

Sheets with cut-off or blurry corner markers will fail to process.

Analytics & Reports

5

Master List: Click the Report Icon located beside the "Add Quiz" button to open the Student's Record.

This view presents a consolidated table of all Students, Assessments, and their corresponding scores.

6

Item Analysis: Inside the assessment list, click the Analysis Icon on a specific quiz.

Profile & Settings

7

Account Management: Access your profile through the top-right dropdown menu to view your credentials.

8

Security Updates: Within the Profile page, you can Update or Change your Current Password at any time to keep your teacher account secure.

Data Protection Active

Passwords are encrypted and scanning data is processed locally using KNN.
