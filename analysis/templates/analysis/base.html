Academic Structure Setup

1

Add Year Level: Use the primary "Add Grade" button to define a new academic year (e.g., 1st Year, 2nd Year).

2

Select Grade/Year: Click on the newly created Grade card or row from your dashboard list to open its specific management view.

3

Manage Sub-categories: Inside the Grade/Year view, a modal will be available to add Sections and Subjects specifically for that level.

This hierarchy ensures teachers only see relevant sections during assessment setup.

Academic Period Setup

4

Add Academic Period: Define the current term (e.g., 1st Semester, 2nd Semester, or Summer Term).

5

Set Duration: Input the Start Date and End Date for the academic period.

Auto-Transition Logic: The system automatically manages active assessments based on these dates. The next academic period will only become available for selection once the current period has reached its End Date.

User Management & Class Assignment

6

Add Teacher: Input the First Name, Last Name, and a unique Username.

Default Credentials: Upon creation, the system automatically assigns the password: @Teacher2025

The Username and this password will be used by the teacher for their initial login.

7

Assign Class: Map the teacher to their specific workload by selecting the Year Level, Subject, and Section.

8

Account Status: Use the Active/Inactive toggle to manage portal access.

  • Active: Teacher can log in and process assessments.
  • Inactive: Account is locked; login attempts will be denied.

Archive & Historical Records

9

Year Transition: Once a new Academic Year is initiated, all data from the previous year is automatically moved to the Archive.

10

Data Retrieval: Select a specific year from the Archive list to view its complete historical footprint:

Stored Records Include:
  • Academic Structure: Year Levels, Sections, and Subjects.
  • Personnel: Teachers assigned to specific subjects during that term.
  • Student Data: Complete class lists and individual profiles.
  • Performance: All assessment records categorized by Academic Period.

Note: Archived records are "Read-Only" to maintain the integrity of past institutional data.
